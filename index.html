Budapest — ELBE Run Transition

Welcome! You're about to go on a scavenger hunt through Budapest — with a twist. Every station teaches you something real about how great teams work.

How it works
1
Find the station. Go to the physical location in the park and find the hidden password card.
2
Unlock it. Enter the password in the app to unlock the station's challenge.
3
Do the game. Read the instructions and complete the activity with your team.
4
Answer the quiz. Guess which principle the game represents. Your answer — right or wrong — gives you a letter. You can retry as many times as you like.
5
Collect all 7 letters. Visit every station and piece together the hidden word to win.
Record a video at every station Film your team performing the activity. You must show all 7 videos to claim your prize — no video, no win!

Each station is locked until you find and enter its password on-site.
